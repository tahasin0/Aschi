## 📋 About The Project

**Aschi** is a lightweight, responsive e-commerce web application built for local grocery delivery. It allows customers to easily browse fresh vegetables, meat, fish, and daily commodities, manage their shopping cart, place orders with Cash on Delivery (COD), and track their delivery status.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🛒 Product Catalog & Categories:** Browse items across multiple categories such as *Raw Market (কাঁচাবাজার)*, *Vegetables (সবজি)*, *Fish (মাছ)*, *Meat (মাংস)*, and *Daily Needs (ডেইলি নিডস)*.
- **🔍 Search & Filtering:** Quick product search and detailed product views with pricing details and unit measurements (kg, liters, pcs).
- **🛍️ Cart & Checkout:** Add/remove items to a dynamic shopping cart with automated subtotal calculations and free delivery thresholds (Free delivery on orders above ৳1,000).
- **👤 User Authentication:** Secure user registration, login, and profile account management to view past orders.
- **📦 Order Tracking:** Customers can track their active orders and delivery status.
- **💵 Cash on Delivery (COD):** Pay conveniently when the order arrives.
- **💬 Direct Support:** Integrated WhatsApp quick-chat support button for customer assistance.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Optimized for both mobile and desktop browsing.
